No other third-party source, SDK, binary, or game asset is included in the public repository. A portable Blender 5.2.2 tool and an unlicensed community NMF add-on archive are kept in private research storage. The add-on was run once as a script against a copied local model in a background Blender process; it is not installed as a general Blender add-on or included here. Build outputs live in ignored local directories and are not committed.
